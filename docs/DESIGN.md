## Brand & Style
The design system caters to an elite, data-driven culinary intelligence platform. It balances high-density information architecture with the sensory allure of gastronomy. 

The aesthetic is precision dark-mode engineered: deep space-grade slates, razor-thin luminous borders, and focused chromatic accents inspired by culinary energy—crimson fire, fresh botanical greens, and amber heat. Rather than whimsical consumer app styling, it presents restaurant discovery with the analytical rigor, high signal-to-noise ratio, and tactile polish of high-end developer dashboards and luxury editorial interfaces.

Key characteristics:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** True dark canvas punctuated by structured, layered surfaces.
- **Precision Typography:** Monospaced and utilitarian pairings that treat gastronomic metrics (flavor profiles, table latency, taste match scores) as first-class computational data.
- **Subtle Luminescence:** Targeted crimson and coral halos that guide attention without washing out critical contrast.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a strictly tiered dark scale paired with purposeful semantic indicators:

- **Canvas & Background:** `#0D1117` provides a low-emission, deep foundation that preserves ocular comfort during low-light usage.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** `#161B22` defines primary containers, creating immediate structural boundaries against the canvas.
- **Interactive & Elevated States:** `#21262D` serves as the resting state for active elements and the hover state for secondary cards.
- **Structural Outlines:** `#30363D` provides crisp structural delineation without jarring contrast.
- **Brand Accents:**
  - Primary Crimson (`#E23744`): Primary calls to action, high-confidence AI badges, and focused focal rings.
  - Coral Flare (`#FF4D5A`): Interactive states, active toggles, and glow diffusions.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - Emerald Green (`#238636`): Verified restaurant ratings, positive sentiment, and open operational statuses.
  - Warning Amber (`#D29922` and `#F0883E`): High-demand alerts, waitlist indicators, and dynamic price surge metrics.
- **Text Palette:**
  - Primary: `#F0F6FC` (headers, metric values, prominent titles).
  - Secondary/Body: `#C9D1D9` (core readable copy, metadata titles).
  - Muted/Meta: `#8B949E` (secondary labels, table captions, inactive states).
  - AI Synthetic Stream: `#E6EDF3` (AI reasoning, natural language breakdowns, prompt previews).

## Typography
Typography reinforces analytical clarity and speed:

- **Inter** acts as the primary visual backbone, deployed with tightened tracking across headlines to maintain an authoritative, technical presentation.
- **JetBrains Mono** handles computational telemetry: match coefficients, live distance metrics, time windows, and AI-driven explanations.
- **AI Explanation Blocks** use the monospaced label family with slight italicization or distinct syntax indentation to instantly distinguish machine-generated insights from human reviews and editorial summaries.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for density without visual friction:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Supports persistent multi-pane layouts: left rail for algorithmic parameters/filters, center stage for contextual restaurant streams, and right drawer for deep predictive inspection.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px margins. Predictive panels collapse into bottom-sheet modals or slide-over surfaces.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margins. High-density vertical stacking prioritizing immediate restaurant discovery cards with horizontal swipe ribbons for quick-filter chips.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation eschews blurry skeuomorphic drop shadows in favor of structured surface boundaries, directional ambient rim-lighting, and targeted emissive halos:

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** `#0D1117` — completely non-elevated ground plane.
- **Layer 1 (Card/Container):** `#161B22` — bounded by a crisp `1px solid #30363D` border. Shadow: none.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated/Hover State):** `#21262D` — border changes to `rgba(240, 246, 252, 0.15)`. Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Layer 3 (Popovers, Tooltips, Context Menus):** `#161B22` — border `1px solid rgba(226, 55, 68, 0.3)`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
- **Emissive Crimson Glow:** Applied exclusively to high-confidence match highlights, critical CTAs, and active AI analysis states:
  - Standard CTA Glow: `0 0 16px rgba(226, 55, 68, 0.35)`
  - Active Pulse Indicator: `0 0 24px rgba(255, 77, 90, 0.25)`

## Shapes
The shape strategy balances engineered geometry with approachable soft interaction zones:

- **Cards & Structural Containers:** Strictly calibrated to `12px` border radius, creating clean modular cohesion across dense dashboards.
- **Input Fields, Action Buttons, & Row Elements:** Calibrated to `8px` border radius, providing solid visual leverage without appearing bulbous.
- **Pills, Match Tags, & Filter Chips:** Full capsule execution at `9999px` border radius, creating distinct contrast against rectangular analytical content.

- **Container:** Background `#161B22`, border `1px solid #30363D`, radius `12px`, overflow hidden.
- **Interactive Hover:** Card border shifts to `#8B949E` with subtle upward transform (`-2px`) and background step-up to `#21262D`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Base Box/Circle:** Size `16px`, background `#0D1117`, border `1px solid #30363D`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or `9999px` (radio).
- **Checked State:** Background `#E23744`, border `1px solid #FF4D5A`, check icon/dot in `#F0F6FC`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Taste Profile Match Gauge:** Circular or horizontal linear meter displaying percentage compatibility (e.g., `98.4% Match`), featuring a high-density track in `#21262D` and a filled value in gradient `#E23744` to `#FF4D5A`.
- **Real-Time Booking Urgency Tag:** Amber notification chip with background `rgba(210, 153, 34, 0.15)`, border `1px solid #D29922`, text `#F0883E`, indicating live seat scarcity or surge intervals.
